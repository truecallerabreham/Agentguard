Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AgentGuard's safety, privacy, and e-commerce integrations.

Will the AI hallucinate fake policies or promises?

No. Unlike generic chatbots, AgentGuard uses a 4-agent pipeline featuring an independent Critic Agent. The Critic programmatically audits the draft against your ground-truth store facts and policies before transmitting anything to the customer. Any hallucination is rejected and revised.

Can customers snoop on another person's order details?

No. Our e-commerce privacy layer enforces strict customer verification: an inquiry for order #1001 requires the customer's matching billing email. Mismatched lookups are denied immediately, and the security event is logged in your immutable audit vault.

Can the AI issue refunds without my permission?

Never. Any financial action exceeding your custom threshold (e.g. $50) is quarantined in your Merchant Approval Inbox. Refunds are only executed when a merchant clicks "Approve" using single-use cryptographic tokens.

How do I install the widget on my store?

Simply copy your 1-line script tag from the Merchant Dashboard and paste it into your Shopify theme.liquid, WooCommerce footer, or custom HTML. It has zero npm dependencies and loads asynchronously without slowing down your storefront.
