Ask us anything
Can the AI hallucinate unauthorized refunds or incorrect return windows?+

No. Unlike generic conversational chatbots, AgentGuard uses a deterministic 4-agent pipeline. Any refund over $50 is quarantined in your Merchant Approval Inbox and requires a single-use cryptographic token signed by you.

How do I embed the chat widget on Shopify or WooCommerce?+

In your Merchant Dashboard, copy your 1-line script tag (e.g. <script src=".../widget.js" data-store-id="..."></script>) and paste it before the closing </body> tag in your Shopify theme.liquid or WooCommerce footer. It has zero npm dependencies.

Is AgentGuard really 100% free forever?+

Yes. AgentGuard is completely free for store owners. There are no credit card requirements, no trial countdowns, and no limits on custom knowledge base policies.

Can I bring my own Google Gemini API key?+

Yes! In your environment settings on Render or locally, set GEMINI_API_KEY=AIzaSy... and AgentGuard will automatically use your key for multi-agent reasoning, or fall back to high-fidelity deterministic responses.
